Privacy Policy

CopyWell records what you copy, which is about as private as data gets. This page explains exactly where it goes.

Last updated 25 September 2026. Applies to CopyWell for macOS.

The short version

Your clipboard history is stored on your Mac. The developer of CopyWell never receives it, cannot read it, and operates no server that it is sent to. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

What CopyWell stores

To do its job, CopyWell keeps a local history of items you copy. Depending on what you copied, an entry may contain:

  • the copied text, link, or image;
  • text recognised inside a copied image, so you can search it;
  • the name of the app you copied from;
  • the time you copied it, and how often you have reused it;
  • tags CopyWell worked out for the item, such as “email address” or “code”.

This is held in a database inside the app's own sandboxed container on your Mac, at ~/Library/Containers/com.copywell.app. Only CopyWell and you can reach it.

What CopyWell deliberately does not store

  • Passwords and secrets. When a password manager such as 1Password, Bitwarden, or Keychain Access marks a copy as confidential, macOS attaches a marker to it. CopyWell honours that marker and does not record the item at all.
  • Anything that looks like a credential. Items that match the shape of an API key, a private key, or a labelled secret are skipped even without a marker.
  • Your keystrokes. CopyWell does not monitor the keyboard and does not use macOS Accessibility features. It never presses keys on your behalf.
  • Your screen, unless you ask. CopyWell never looks at the screen on its own. It captures it only at the moment you use its screenshot or screen recording command.

Screenshots and screen recordings

These two features need macOS's Screen Recording permission, which CopyWell asks for the first time you use one of them. You can withdraw it at any time in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording; the clipboard history keeps working without it.

  • A screenshot is taken only when you press the shortcut or choose the command, and only the area you select is kept. Copying it puts it on the clipboard and in your history like anything else you copy; saving it writes a file where you choose.
  • A recording starts only when you press Record and stops when you press Stop. It is saved to the Movies ▸ CopyWell folder on your Mac. The Mac's sound is included only if you switch that on.
  • Your camera and microphone are used only if you switch on the camera bubble or your voice for recordings. Both are off to begin with, and macOS asks for your permission the first time you turn one on. They are active only while a recording is being made, and what they capture goes into that recording on your Mac and nowhere else.
  • Screenshots and recordings are processed on your Mac. They are not uploaded anywhere, and images are never included in iCloud sync.

Data the developer collects

None. CopyWell contains no analytics framework, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that transmits data. The app makes network connections for exactly two purposes, both to Apple:

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off until you switch it on in Settings. When it is on, clipboard items are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. That database belongs to you:

  • The developer of CopyWell has no access to it and cannot read its contents.
  • Items marked confidential, and anything CopyWell judged to be a secret, are never uploaded — they stay on the Mac that captured them.
  • Apple's handling of the data is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Turning sync off stops further uploads. Deleting an item deletes it from iCloud and from your other Macs.

Text recognition in images

When you copy an image, CopyWell reads any text in it so the image can be found by searching. This uses Apple's Vision framework and runs entirely on your Mac. The image is not uploaded for processing.

How long data is kept

You decide. CopyWell can keep history indefinitely, or delete items automatically after an age you choose. If your subscription lapses the app locks and stops recording, but nothing already saved is deleted — it stays on your Mac. You can delete any item, or clear the entire history, at any time from within the app. Deleting is immediate and permanent.

Children

CopyWell is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to request, export, or erase from a server. Everything CopyWell knows is on your own Mac and under your control: the app can export your history to JSON, CSV, Markdown, or HTML, and can delete it entirely.
